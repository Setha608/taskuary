# Retail & e-commerce

For operators of retail stores, online shops and omnichannel businesses in the US. These businesses sell products through physical stores, websites, marketplaces and other sales channels, and coordinate merchandising, inventory, fulfillment, payments and customer service.

**With this pack, your agents know:**

- **The language.** SKU, POS, GMV, AOV, sell-through, shrink, replenishment, BOPIS and chargebacks. They recognize common retail terms and abbreviations in work messages. → `glossary.md`
- **The software.** Which point-of-sale, e-commerce, inventory, order management, warehouse, shipping and customer service systems businesses commonly use, and what each is for. → `software.md`
- **The big players.** Major retailers and marketplaces, commerce technology platforms, industry associations and relevant public agencies. → `players.md`
- **How the year runs.** Daily sales reconciliation, month-end reporting, inventory planning, back-to-school periods, holiday promotions, Black Friday and post-holiday returns. → `conventions.md`

It is reference, not advice. Agents use it to understand messages and look things up. Retail practices, tax rules and regulations vary, so for anything with a date, amount or legal consequence, an agent checks the current authoritative source before acting on it.
